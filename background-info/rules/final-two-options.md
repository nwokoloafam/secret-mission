# Final 2 Options

What happens when only 2 players are left. They're already hunting each other in the loop.
**Status:** undecided. Build option 1 as the default; others can be added later.

Constraint: it's a background game, so no spectacle or crowd involvement.

## Still in the running
1. **Just keep going (default)**
   Nothing changes. Normal missions until one lands, or the end time hits and most kills wins.
   Build cost: none.

2. **Silent final** (shortlisted)
   Neither knows they're in the final 2. Everyone sees only "2 agents remain"; the two players get no special notice.
   Keeps paranoia high. Combines with option 1.
   Build cost: tiny (suppress the "final 2" notification for those two players).

3. **Double-or-nothing**
   Normal missions, but the winner also takes the loser's kill count for the tally.
   Build cost: small (scoring tweak).

4. **Survive the clock**
   At final 2, a timer starts (say 30 min). Land your mission and you win; if neither does, most kills wins.
   Build cost: small (one timer, respects pause).

## Rejected (too much spectacle or too hard)
- **Same mission race:** both get the identical mission; first to land it wins. Rejected as too tough.
- **Unmasked duel:** both are told who the other is and get fresh missions.
- **Crowd's choice:** ghosts vote on the final mission from 3 options. Rejected: crowd voting is overkill for a background game.
