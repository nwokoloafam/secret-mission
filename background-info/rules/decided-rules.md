# Decided Rules

Locked-in changes on top of `../plan-overview.md`. This is the source of truth for the build.

## Host
- **Afam is referee only, not a player.** He has admin and settles disputes. No conflict of interest.

## Winning
- **Most kills among survivors wins** when the game ends (end time or last one standing).
- **Tiebreak:** the survivor who reached that kill count first.
- Correct counter-accusations count as kills (see below).
- Final 2 format: not decided. Build "just keep going" as the default; see `final-two-options.md`.

## Ending the game
- **Target end time + host control.** A target end time (e.g. 2.5 hours after Start) shows on everyone's screen, with a "30 minutes left" notice.
- Afam can end the game early or extend the end time from admin.
- Pause time doesn't count toward the clock.

## Leavers
- **Saying bye:** Afam taps **Remove** in admin. Their hunter inherits their target with a new mission.
- **"I'm leaving" button:** on the player page, for people who forget to say bye. It removes them and pings Afam.
- **Silent leavers:** a hunter taps **Can't find them**, Afam checks, and removes them if they're gone.
- Removing a player is **not** a kill: no one gets kill credit.
- Removed players show up in the end reveal as "left early."

## Kill stories
- **Dropped.** No "tell the story" prompt after a catch (decided Nov 15). The reveal shows the kill chain without stories.

## Late arrivals
- **No time limit to join.** Late arrivals tap "I'm here" and join the loop at the next catch (or within 5 minutes).
- **Joining closes at the final 6.** Once 6 or fewer players are left, new arrivals become spectators (they can bet and send a tip). Decided Nov 15; replaces the original "~5 players or 90 minutes" cutoff.

## Texts
- **No text messages** (decided Nov 15). US carrier registration could take up to two weeks, too close to the party. The sign-up form no longer asks for a phone number. Afam announces the start in person and with the Partiful blast.

## Drinks
- **Every catch earns a beer, a shot, or a mocktail on Afam's bar tab.** Correct accusations count as catches.
- **Honor system:** players tell the bartender; nothing in the app tracks it. (A phone voucher with a "Redeem" button was considered and parked for later.)
- **Afam can close the tab** from the admin page ("Close the tab"). Every player then sees a banner that the tab is finished; catches still count. He can reopen it.
- No task ever requires drinking.

## Missions
- **Task swaps are part of the bonus move** (see below). Self-serve, no need to ask Afam.
- **Afam's skip is the backstop:** if a player has used their bonus move and is still stuck (target left, mission doesn't fit), Afam can skip the mission from admin.

## Pause
- Afam can pause and resume from admin.
- While paused: kill-code entry is rejected, grace-period and splice-fallback timers freeze, every screen shows a "Game paused" banner.

## Moving a player (targets too close)
- Admin action **Move** on any player: pull them out of the loop and splice them in at a random spot (not next to the person they were too close to).
- Swapping two hunters' targets is **not** allowed: in a single loop it splits it into two loops.
- Any player can ask Afam for this ("my target is my roommate").

## Code leaking
- Ignored. Someone could tell a friend their code early, but it's unlikely at a friendly party.

## Bonus move (decided Nov 16)
- Each player gets **one bonus move per game**. Using it on either option uses it up:
  1. **Swap your task.**
  2. **Guess your hunter.** Pick who you think has you as their target.
     - **Right:** your hunter is out and it counts as your catch; their hunter now hunts you. (You also get the 10-minute shield, as before. Open question: keep it?)
     - **Wrong:** nothing happens, except the bonus move is gone.
  3. *(Draft, not built)* **Team up.** See `team-up-draft.md`.
- Replaces: 1 accusation + 1 bonus accusation at 3 catches, a separate task swap, and hints to the hunter on a wrong guess.

## Custom hints
- Afam can write a **custom hint for each player** from admin, before or during the game.
- Afam can send any player's custom hint to their hunter, at any time (e.g. "they changed shirts").
- Automatic hints were dropped (Nov 16): the hunter already has your name and photo, so clues added little.

## Ghosts (eliminated players)
- **Betting pool:** ghosts pick who they think will win; correct picks get a shout-out at the reveal.
- **Stats board ("Who's winning"):** ghosts see each remaining player's catch count, to bet with. Live players still can't see counts.
- **Poltergeist:** each ghost can send one anonymous hint to any living player, true or a red herring.

## Not doing
- Couple/best-friend avoidance in the shuffle (use Move instead).
- Crowd voting or anything that turns the game into a spectacle.
