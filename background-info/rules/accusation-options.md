# Counter-Accusation Options

> **Superseded (Nov 16):** guessing your hunter is now one choice of the single **bonus move** (see `decided-rules.md`). A wrong guess does nothing except use the bonus move; there are no automatic hints and no extra guess at 3 catches. Kept below as the record of what was considered.

## Decided
- 1 accusation per game, plus 1 bonus at 3 kills (adjustable).
- Correct: hunter eliminated, their hunter now hunts you.
- Correct boost: **counts as a kill + 10-minute shield.**
- Shield blocks kill codes only, not accusations.
- Correct accusations count toward the 3-kill bonus.
- Wrong penalty: **hint to your hunter.**
- Afam can write a custom hint per player.

## Wrong-accusation penalty options (all considered)
| Option | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Hint to your hunter** | Your hunter gets a clue about you | **Picked** |
| Exposed | For 10 min your hunter sees "they're onto you" | Not picked |
| Lose your reroll | You lose your next (or only) mission reroll | Not picked |
| Locked out | You can't enter kill codes for 5 min | Not picked |

## Correct-accusation boost options (all considered)
| Option | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Counts as a kill** | Adds to your tally for most-kills | **Picked** |
| **Shield** | Immune for 10 min while your new hunter catches up | **Picked** |
| Free reroll | Get your reroll back or an extra one | Not picked |
| Intel | Hint about who your new hunter is | Not picked |

## How hints are picked
Hints come from data the game already has, plus one tiny check-in step. Never the full name or photo.

**Hint sources**
- ~~**Group:** "how they know Afam" from sign-up.~~ Dropped: already on the target card.
- **Outfit:** at check-in, the player taps their top color and one accessory (glasses / hat / none). Two taps, no typing. Medium.
- **Initial:** first letter of first name. Strong.
- **Custom (per player):** Afam writes a hint for each player from admin, before or during the game ("loudest laugh in the room", "wearing Afam's old jacket"). Optional; players without one skip that tier.

**Escalation**
Each wrong accusation by the same player gives the next tier up:
1. Outfit: "Your target is wearing black"
2. Custom: Afam's hint for that player (skipped if none)
3. Initial: "Your target's name starts with M"

"How they know Afam" was dropped as a tier during testing (Nov 12): the target card already shows it, so it gave no new information.

If a tier would reveal too little (e.g. 15 people from college), skip to the next. The hunter gets the hint as a notification and on their target card.

**Manual send:** Afam can push any player's custom hint to their hunter at any time from admin (e.g. to break a stalemate).

**Edge cases**
- If the hunter already knows their target well, the hint does nothing. That's fine; it's a soft penalty.
- Hints persist on the hunter's card until they get a new target.
- Ghost poltergeist hints can use the same sources, or be free text.
