# Ghost Mode Options

What eliminated players (and post-cutoff late arrivals) can do. Goal: keep them invested without pulling focus from the party.

## Picked
- **Betting pool:** ghosts pick who they think will win. Correct picks get a shout-out at the end reveal.
- **Poltergeist:** each ghost can send one anonymous hint to any living player, true or a red herring.

## Not picked yet (kept for later)
- **Revival mission:** ghosts earn one way back with a hard mission. They rejoin like a late arrival, no grace period.
- **Bounty council:** ghosts vote every 20 min on who gets a bounty. (Crowd voting; probably too much for a background game.)
- **Witnesses:** ghosts can be called on to help Afam settle disputes.
- **Story judges:** only ghosts vote on best kill story. (Voting; may be too much.)
- **Informants:** sell hints to living players (from the original plan, for late arrivals after the join cutoff). Needs a currency to work.
- **Side missions:** fun missions with no game effect, just for the reveal.
