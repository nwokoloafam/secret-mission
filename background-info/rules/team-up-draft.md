# Team Up (draft, not built)

A third option for the **bonus move**. Kept as a draft until Rads decides whether to include it.

## As described (Nov 16)
- Two players can **team up**. Both must opt in; there are no notifications about it.
- Teammates **share targets**: each hunts both their own target and their teammate's target.
- **If one teammate is caught, both are out.**
- They're **stuck together** until both targets are caught. Neither moves on to a new target until then.
- **If you try to team up with your own target, you're out.**

## How it would likely work (my reading, to confirm)
- **Opting in:** A taps "Team up" and picks B. Nothing visible happens until B also picks A, at which point the team forms. The request uses A's bonus move; accepting uses B's.
- **Catching:** either teammate can enter the code of either team target.
- **After both targets are caught:** the team splits and each teammate goes back to hunting normally (each gets the next target along their part of the loop).
- **One teammate caught:** whoever caught them gets the catch; the other teammate is out too (does the catcher get credit for both?).

## Open questions
1. If A asks to team up with their own target, A is out. What if A asks to team up with **their hunter**? (A doesn't know who that is.) Allowed, not allowed, or something else?
2. When one teammate is caught, does the catcher get **2 catches** (both teammates), or 1?
3. Does asking to team up use your bonus move **even if the other person never accepts**?
4. Can a request be cancelled, and does it expire?
5. Can teams form during the final few players, or is there a cutoff?
6. Do teammates see each other's targets on their own card (two photos, two tasks)?
