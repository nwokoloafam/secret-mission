# Missions (draft list)

Guests-only, low-noise, built to make people mix. No bar staff or strangers, no drinking required.
The killer completes the mission, then tells the target "got you" and asks for their code.

Written theme-neutral; theme flavor gets added later in the copy.

**This file is the working list for now.** Edit it freely. When the platform is built, missions move into the database and get edited from the admin page (see `../build-notes.md`); this file then becomes the seed data.
**Difficulty:** E = easy, M = medium, H = hard. Starting missions should be E/M; H is for later (revival, final stretch).

## Get them to say something
1. (E) Get them to say "birthday."
2. (E) Get them to say the name of a country.
3. (E) Get them to say "Afam."
4. (M) Get them to say "pineapple."
5. (M) Get them to say a number bigger than 100.
6. (M) Get them to say the name of a food from your childhood.
7. (M) Get them to say "no way."
8. (H) Get them to say "abeg."
9. (H) Get them to sing any line of a song.
10. (H) Get them to say "Happy birthday" in a language other than English.

## Learn something about them
11. (E) Learn their middle name.
12. (E) Learn where they grew up.
13. (E) Learn their go-to karaoke song.
14. (M) Learn the name of their first pet (or that they never had one).
15. (M) Learn what they wanted to be when they were 8.
16. (M) Learn the last thing they cooked.
17. (M) Learn how they actually met Afam (the full story, not just "college").
18. (M) Learn the most-used emoji on their phone.
19. (H) Learn their most embarrassing moment with Afam.
20. (H) Learn the worst job they've ever had.

## Get a photo
21. (E) Get a selfie with them.
22. (M) Get a photo of them holding a spoon.
23. (M) Get a photo of them pointing at Afam.
24. (M) Get a photo of them doing a thumbs up.
25. (H) Get a photo of them mid-laugh.
26. (H) Get a photo of the two of you with a third guest, all making the same face.

## Get them to do something
27. (E) Get them to high-five you.
28. (E) Get them to show you a photo on their phone.
29. (M) Get them to tell you a joke.
30. (M) Get them to rate Afam's outfit out of 10.
31. (M) Get them to teach you a word in another language.
32. (M) Get them to fist-bump someone they just met.
33. (M) Get them to recommend you a show or movie.
34. (H) Get them to do a dance move, even a small one.
35. (H) Get them to switch seats or spots with you.
36. (H) Get them to introduce you to someone you don't know.

## Get them to agree or debate
37. (E) Get them to agree that Afam looks great tonight.
38. (M) Get them to pick a side: jollof rice, Nigerian or Ghanaian.
39. (M) Get them to name their top 3 artists right now.
40. (M) Get them to settle a debate: pineapple on pizza, yes or no.
41. (H) Get them to give a 10-second birthday speech for Afam.
42. (H) Get them to admit something Afam has done that annoyed them.

## Notes
- Some H missions (singing, dancing, speeches) are louder than "low-noise"; keep them for late game or cut them.
- #8, #10, #38 lean into the Nigerian theme; they work with any theme too.
- Target: 30–50 total. Add, cut, or reword freely.
